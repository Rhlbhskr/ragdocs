Benefits Guide — January 2026
This fictional guide is included for demonstration only.

Time off
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave each calendar year, prorated for their start date. Up to five unused days may carry into the next calendar year and must be used by March 31. Request planned leave at least ten business days in advance. Employees receive ten days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

Learning and development
Employees have an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books, professional certifications, and conferences relevant to their role. Manager approval is required before purchase. Unused learning budget does not carry over into the following year. Employees can use up to three paid learning days annually.

Wellbeing
The company reimburses up to $50 per month for eligible fitness and wellbeing subscriptions. Submit a receipt by the fifth day of the following month. This benefit is separate from the home office equipment allowance.
